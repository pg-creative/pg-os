# PG OS — /go-crazy Output (2026-04-27)

## Current state

PG OS is a Next.js 15 / React 19 personal command center at `127.0.0.1:3030`. Five tabs, floating Capture FAB with voice, three Laputa palettes that auto-switch by hour. Phases 1-8 + Recursive Fox A/B/C/D shipped: Supabase backend (encrypted tokens, queue + claude mirrors), ⌘J streaming AI co-pilot with nine tools, ⌘⇧M brand-mode cycling, morning/evening RitualGates. OAuth wired to Google, Spotify, Whoop. Ghibli + golden hour + JRPG, locked. The bones are real. Below: where it could go if no one was watching.

---

## 10 ideas that would make people lose their minds

### 1. The Pre-Cog Stage Manager
Whoop HRV + calendar density + Spotify tempo + last 30 min of git/transcript activity feed a small local model that **predicts the next context-switch 90 seconds out** and silently pre-stages it: opens Ghostty, primes the right Claude project, queues the three files most likely to be touched, tunes Spotify to the focus mode that historically worked at this HRV. PG arrives and the OS is already there.
**Insane because:** the cursor moves before he does. Interface becomes a stagehand who watches him think.
**Reference:** Microsoft Copilot Work IQ 2026 — proactive surfacing. PG OS would be the local-first single-user version.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 6 weeks. Whoop + Calendar wired. Need a tiny on-device sequence model + macOS Shortcuts bridge.

### 2. Plural — Conversations With Past PGs
Your 1,258 indexed Claude conversations + journal entries + meeting transcripts + LinkedIn posts get sliced into **temporal personas**: "PG of Q4 2024," "PG mid-Metrasens-launch," "PG-during-Voyager-pivot." Each is a queryable agent with its own embeddings, voice, and beliefs. Open the ⌘J panel, pick Past-PG-Oct-2024, ask "what would you do here?" Get an answer that actually disagrees with present-PG.
**Insane because:** the Cognitive Audit headline ("you are plural") becomes literal. You can argue with prior selves and watch them be wrong about you.
**Reference:** A-Mem (agentic memory, Feb 2025) + LoCoMo benchmark for long-horizon conversational memory.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 3 weeks. claude-archive is already FTS5-indexed.

### 3. The Laputa Window
Home stops being cards and becomes a **single living frame** — a generative Ghibli landscape at 24fps reflecting hour, HRV, calendar density, weather. Slow recovery + heavy day = autumn rain on copper rooftops. High strain + clear evening = airships drifting through golden cumulus. Habits show as scene glyphs (a streak is a lit lantern). Capture FAB becomes a paper crane that lands on your wrist.
**Insane because:** the dashboard *is* the world. You read your day the way you'd read a sky.
**Reference:** Generative UI 2026 + WebGPU + react-three-fiber. SDXL Turbo on M-series hits 24fps at 512px.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 8 weeks. Heaviest on the list — also the one people would screenshot and post.

### 4. Confession Booth Mode
Every evening at 9:42pm (your statistical wind-down), the OS dims to Midnight palette and the Capture FAB pulses once. Tap and you get a single voice prompt: *"What was true today that you didn't say out loud?"* The answer gets transcribed, encrypted, written to a sealed `journal/confessional/` table, and **never shown back to you** unless you explicitly ask via a typed phrase. The OS uses it silently — embeddings only — to tune the next morning's RitualGate prompt.
**Insane because:** the system holds things you wouldn't write down. It listens and acts on it without ever quoting you back. Therapy-grade trust ritual encoded in software.
**Reference:** Reflective Memory Management (Mar 2025) — prospective reflection patterns that summarize without surfacing.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 1 week. Trivially small. Hardest part is the trust contract.

### 5. The Standing Council
⌘⇧C summons a **panel of three advisors in real-time** — not generic LLMs, but persona-tuned agents built from your own corpus: The Operator (Metrasens-PG, ROI-brained), The Maker (Alchmy-PG, taste-led), The Writer (literary-PG, slow + skeptical). They argue with each other on screen about whatever's in your clipboard or current Notion page. You watch the disagreement, then pick. Every pick trains who gets louder next time.
**Insane because:** PG already runs this council in his head. Externalizing it means he can SEE which voice is winning and notice when one's been quiet for a month.
**Reference:** Multi-agent LLM debate frameworks (CAMEL, AutoGen 2026 patterns) + the retired llm-council skill, repurposed as an internal panel.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 2 weeks. Streaming three Anthropic calls in parallel + a debate-orchestration tool.

### 6. Time Fold
Scrub a horizontal "fold" handle at the top of any view and **time bends.** Drag left: Home as it looked on this date last year — same widgets, real data, streak, projects, ships. Drag right: 3-month / 6-month forward projection extrapolated from ship velocity, recovery, habit slopes. Decisions made with past visible behind, cone-of-futures in front.
**Insane because:** every dashboard is a snapshot of NOW. This is a 4D object you rotate through time.
**Reference:** No consumer product ships this. Closest: Tableau time-slider + Apple Photos memories.
**Feasibility:** 4 weeks. ships.db + habits + journal already temporal.

### 7. Wraith Mode (Cross-Brand Possession)
⌘⇧M cycles brand modes today. Wraith Mode **possesses the whole OS by brand voice.** Alchmy mode: co-pilot talks royal-purple, cinematic-literary, no cute. Voyager: patient gaming-criticism prose. Writer: gets quiet, signs replies "PG", rejects bullets. Capture destinations re-rank, RitualGate questions change, Spotify shifts to the brand's audio palette.
**Insane because:** instead of switching apps, PG switches *selves* — the OS becomes that self with him.
**Reference:** Persona-conditioned generative UI. Inverted character.ai — user is the character, OS is the supporting cast.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 2 weeks. Brand mode infra exists; this is config + system-prompt expansion.

### 8. The Ledger of Almosts
Every queue item PG declines, every proposal he rejects, every Hero's Chronicle quest he abandons gets logged to a "Ledger of Almosts" that surfaces once a quarter as a single page: *here are the 47 things you almost did, ranked by how often the world has since validated the bet.* It's a **graveyard with receipts.** The OS uses it to recalibrate its own proposal confidence.
**Insane because:** most tools celebrate what you did. This one shows what you didn't, scored by the universe. Rare honesty.
**Reference:** Decision-journaling literature (Annie Duke) + Anthropic's proposals_log table (already in PG OS schema).
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 1 week. Schema exists. Just needs the quarterly view + outcome scraper.

### 9. Ambient Composing
Every text box in PG OS has a hidden second mode: tap-and-hold the FAB and the field becomes a **continuous voice canvas**. As PG talks, the OS streams transcription + simultaneously generates a Ghibli ink-wash background that morphs to the emotional register of what he's saying. Pause, and a faint margin agent suggests one parenthetical. Speak again, it dissolves. The page feels alive without ever interrupting.
**Insane because:** writing surfaces today are dead rectangles. This one *breathes* with you. Closest existing thing is Granola's silent transcription, but visualized.
**Reference:** OpenAI Atlas OS ambient interface patterns + Whisper streaming + SDXL Turbo background gen.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 5 weeks.

### 10. The Sleep-Time Agent
While PG sleeps (Whoop confirms), a fleet of background agents **lives the day he didn't have time for**: drafts the LinkedIn post from the conversation that died at 11:47pm, writes the three flagged-not-opened emails, files the Notion page from the 4pm transcript, queues a carousel for the Alchmy idea voiced into Capture and forgotten. He wakes to a Notion page titled *Last Night's Work* — drafts with one-line "why I did this" + Approve/Discard.
**Insane because:** the OS has a second shift. The day expands by working when he can't.
**Reference:** Copilot Cowork (Apr 2026) background execution + existing Anthropic streaming + queueStore.
**Feasibility (unlimited):** 3 weeks. Hardest part is the cost gate.

## 3 'cheap to test, high upside' ideas

1. **The Ledger of Almosts (#8)** — purely additive. Add one column to `decisions_log`, build one quarterly view. Could ship Saturday afternoon. Pays back forever.
2. **Confession Booth Mode (#4)** — one new Capture destination, one encrypted table, one nightly cron. <200 lines. The trust contract is the work; the code is small.
3. **The Standing Council (#5)** — three parallel Anthropic streams in the existing ⌘J panel + three system prompts hand-tuned from Alchmy / Voyager / Writer voice locks. No new infra. Weekend prototype. Likely the most-used feature within a month.

## 1 'this might break my brain' idea

**The Laputa Window (#3).** Killing the dashboard. Replacing Home with a single living Ghibli frame whose every pixel is a data signal. No cards, no charts, no streaks-as-numbers — the OS becomes a window you stand in front of, and your day reads back to you as weather. People wouldn't believe it was a productivity tool. They'd think it was art. It would be both. It would also be the most-screenshotted personal-software artifact of 2026, and the moment "personal OS" stops sounding like a category and starts sounding like a *world.*
