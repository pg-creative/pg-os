# Cross-Module Tiers — Multi-Dimensional Rewards

> Tiers that require **simultaneous progress across modules** to unlock. These are the rewards that gamify the *system*, not any single number. The whole point: $15K MTD with broken sleep and zero workouts is a worse outcome than $10K MTD with high recovery and weekly Yuriko chapters.
>
> Single-axis revenue tier rewards live in `../upwork/revenue-tiers.md`.
>
> **Beta-testing plan:** these 4 tiers are also being set up as goals INSIDE Hero's Chronicle (PG's life-RPG app, launching Oct 2 2026). PG dogfoods HC by tracking these compound goals there → finds UX gaps → ships fixes pre-launch.
>
> Locked 2026-05-15.

## How this works
- Tier is **unlocked** when ALL conditions held in the same calendar month
- Tier is **earned** when held for 2+ consecutive months
- Tier is **lost** if any condition drops for a full month (re-earn from scratch)

## Tier C1: "The Setup Works"

**Conditions (all in the same month):**
- $5K MTD income
- 3+ workouts/week (4 of 4 weeks)
- 1+ creative piece shipped on any brand
- Sleep consistency > 70%

**Reward: Buy yourself a creative-only day**

*One full day, no Upwork, no Claude, no email. Yuriko OR essay OR game dev ONLY. The reward IS the day. Books within 14 days of crossing the threshold.*

## Tier C2: "Sustained Operator"

**Conditions (all in the same month, NO drops below threshold during the month):**
- **$12K MTD** (raised from $10K — needs to feel harder)
- **4 workouts/week** (raised from 3 — consistency floor)
- **1 Yuriko chapter AND 1 essay/post** (raised from OR — both axes)
- **Sleep consistency > 80%** (raised from >75%)
- **Recovery > 75% on 5+ days/week** (raised from >70%)

**Reward: 5-day solo retreat in a meaningful place**

*Real getaway. Mountain cabin, coastal town, or international short-haul. 5 days, no work, no agent. First 3 days you rest; last 2 you ship one thing on a long-stalled project. Budget $1-2K.*

## Tier C3: "Free Agent"

**Conditions (all in the same month):**
- $15K MTD (GOAL — also the Tier 4 revenue tier)
- 4+ workouts/week sustained
- 1 deep creative piece + 4+ micro-pieces
- Sleep consistency > 80%
- Recovery > 75% on 5+ days/week

**Reward: 2-week sabbatical somewhere meaningful**

*International trip or extended domestic stay. Pre-30th-birthday. Resets you for the final October push. Costs $2-5K. The trip is funded by the system you built.*

## Tier C4: "October Mission"

**Conditions:**
- C3 held for 2 consecutive months by end of September 2026
- Free agency conversation initiated with Metrasens
- Hero's Chronicle live (Oct 2 launch shipped)

**Reward: Gathering — bring people together to mark it**

*Friends + family in one place for a long weekend. Rent a house somewhere good. The reward is the people, not the trip. Late Sept / early Oct 2026, around the 30th birthday.*

## Notes on tier design

- Rewards must be **specific** (named the thing, not "celebrate")
- Rewards must be **reachable** within 2-3 weeks of unlock
- Rewards must be **proportional** (C1 day off ≠ C4 gathering)
- Rewards must be **observable** (PG OS dashboard + HC both surface unlock)
- C2's conditions are deliberately the hardest gate per-axis — proves sustainability before C3 stretches further
- C4 isn't pure income — it has a SHIP requirement (HC launch) and a LIFE requirement (W2 conversation). The 30th birthday is the deadline, not the reward.

## Hero's Chronicle beta-test mapping

PG is setting these up inside HC's existing goal/quest system as a power-user dogfood pass. Each compound tier maps to ONE HC entity. Where HC's model can't represent a condition (e.g. multi-axis AND-gates), that's the UX feedback HC needs before Oct 2 launch.
